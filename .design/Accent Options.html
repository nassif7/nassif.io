nassif.pro · Accent study

Ten accents, one rulebook.

Black type, white ground, hairline rules — fixed. The accent only ever touches four things: the eyebrow labels, the active rule, links, and status pills. Judge them there, not as swatches: an accent that looks great as a square can fall apart at 9px uppercase.

Type: black #000 Ground: white #FFF Accent: ≤5% of surface
Forest
#1E6141

Your pick. Reads considered and quiet — the least common accent in tech, which is exactly why it looks like a studio rather than a startup. Safest of the greens at small sizes.
